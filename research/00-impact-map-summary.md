# Why this tool — Phase 1 impact map summary (14.9.2026)

Method: State Comptroller + Ombudsman reports 2023–2026 on מיצוי זכויות / שירות לאזרח (50 items screened, 22 findings extracted, 12 quantified and ranked by people-in-gap × unfixed × buildable), overlaid with Ombudsman complaint volumes and Kol Zchut 2025 traffic. Seven finalist reports were read in full and every figure below was verified verbatim at the cited printed page.

**The seniors cell won and is the only one where all three sources agree.**

| Finding | Figure | Source (printed page) |

What fell out and why: reservists' self-employed debt (₪37M / 4,400, ch.1 p.24) was cancelled by a late-2024 law and compensation take-up is ~99% (p.19); the Equal Opportunities Commission already runs a routing questionnaire + digital guide for reservists and spouses (p.53). Disabled IDF veterans: 74–80% unaware, but the survey covered the ~15,300 with head/mental/PTSD injuries, not all 60,000 (74א pp. 1475, 1479). Arnona all-groups: confirmed by the Ombudsman's Feb 2026 special report (937 complaints, 41.8% on discounts) but no source counts eligible non-recipients — the seniors' slice is inside this tool.
